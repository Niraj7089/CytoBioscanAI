Multimodal AI Diagnostic Pipeline Architecture

This research prototype integrates high-resolution optical single-cell microscopy with 3-electrode electrochemical biosensing. Optical computer vision captures morphology and structural texture, while electrochemical impedance spectroscopy measures membrane dielectric integrity ($R_{ct}$, $C_{dl}$) and Faradaic redox kinetics.
